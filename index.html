Our Story

A Legacy of Flavors in Bodakdev

Nestled in the vibrant Gulmohar area of Bodakdev, Kamla is more than just a restaurant — it's a celebration of North India's rich culinary heritage. Every dish we serve carries the warmth of traditional recipes, perfected over generations and crafted with the finest spices and freshest ingredients.

From our aromatic biryanis to the silky richness of our dal makhani, each plate tells a story of passion and authenticity. Whether you're stopping by for a quick chai at our café corner or settling in for a full family dinner, Kamla welcomes you with open arms and unforgettable flavors.

Our Specialties

North Indian Classics

Savor the timeless flavors of butter chicken, dal makhani, paneer tikka, and our signature thali — each dish prepared with handground spices and slow-cooked to perfection.

Café Corner

Unwind with our premium café selections — from aromatic masala chai and filter coffee to specialty lattes. The perfect spot for conversations and calm.

Tandoor Specials
